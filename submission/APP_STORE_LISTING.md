## Introduction

Resolve order-support phone work with consent-based AI calls and merchant review.

## Description

CallMeMaybe helps Shopify merchants handle support tasks that still require a
phone call. Customers can explicitly request a call about their order from the
order-status or signed-in customer-account experience. Merchants can also send
an approved AI assistant to a verified carrier support number for a package
trace.

Every customer call is tied to an order, active consent, calling limits, local
calling hours, suppression checks, and a short-lived verification code. CALL-E
returns a structured outcome for merchant review. Consequential order changes
are never authorized by the AI: the merchant approves the proposal and the app
rechecks the live order before any supported Shopify mutation.

## Feature bullets

- Let customers request and revoke an order-support call from Shopify surfaces.
- Verify the recipient before disclosing order information.
- Call verified carrier support numbers with explicit merchant approval.
- Review redacted outcomes and proposed next steps in Shopify Admin.
- Block stale address changes, cancellations, and notes with a fresh order check.
- Track included and overage completed-call usage with a hard monthly ceiling.
- Stop calls globally or by approved regional policy.
- Honor suppression, quiet hours, attempt limits, and spoken opt-out.

## Important limitations and disclosures

- CallMeMaybe is for order support, not marketing, lead generation, collections,
  political calls, emergency services, or purchased lists.
- Returns, refunds, and replacements are captured as merchant-reviewed proposals;
  the app does not automatically issue them.
- A region is available only when CallMeMaybe and CALL-E have completed the
  required legal, caller-ID, routing, and localization approval.
- CallMeMaybe does not store call audio or raw transcripts.
- Failed, busy, unanswered, canceled, and provider-error calls are not billable.
