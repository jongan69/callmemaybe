# Submission checklist

The detailed, evidence-bearing checklist is
[`submission/RELEASE_READINESS.md`](../submission/RELEASE_READINESS.md). Do not
submit from this shorthand list alone.

- [ ] Release commit/tag and CI evidence are final.
- [ ] Production/staging isolation and restore/rollback rehearsal are evidenced.
- [ ] Legal, trademark, vendor, regional, protected-data, and localization gates
      are signed.
- [ ] Shopify App Pricing exactly matches the documented plan and usage handle.
- [ ] All 28 regions remain disabled until their individual approvals are linked.
- [ ] App URLs, OAuth redirects, webhooks, public policies, support, security, and
      status page resolve on the branded domain.
- [ ] Reviewer store, credentials, controlled numbers, test data, and exact steps
      work in incognito.
- [ ] Six screenshots and both videos were captured from the signed candidate.
- [ ] Two people who did not build the feature completed the reviewer walkthrough.
- [ ] Partner Dashboard automated checks pass and emergency contacts are monitored.
