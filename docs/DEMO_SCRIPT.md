# Signed-release demo script

Use synthetic orders and controlled phone numbers. Record the exact signed
candidate after all release gates and reviewer rehearsals pass. Never expose a
password, session token, complete phone number, reusable verification code, or
real customer data.

## Three-minute marketing demo

1. Open the embedded overview: “CallMeMaybe handles order-support phone work
   while keeping consent, policy, billing, and the merchant in control.”
2. Show setup progress, the approved region, business identity, usage, and global
   stop switch. Do not imply unapproved regions are available.
3. Switch to the synthetic buyer order. Show that consent begins unchecked,
   then grant it and show the attempt/expiry/revocation disclosure.
4. In the signed-in customer account, show active consent, select a support
   subject, and request the call. Briefly mask the newly displayed support-code
   area; explain that the code is short-lived and single-use.
5. On the controlled line, demonstrate AI/business disclosure and code
   verification. Return to the case and show the redacted structured proposal.
6. Approve a safe proposal. Show that the app rereads Shopify and blocks a stale
   proposal if the order changed.
7. Start the verified carrier stand-in leg. Show the trace result as a reviewed
   note/proposal, not an automatically executed refund, return, or replacement.
8. Show completed-call usage. Explain that failed/no-answer calls cost zero and
   the app stops at the $200 overage ceiling.
9. Revoke consent and show the next attempt blocked. End on the global stop
   switch and privacy/support links.

## Reviewer screencast

Record the full sequence in `submission/REVIEWER_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, including
installation, pricing approval/decline, all onboarding steps, both call legs,
verification failure, provider failure/no answer, duplicate callback safety,
merchant rejection, order drift, usage, revocation, suppression, and kill switch.
Keep the recording unedited where continuity helps the reviewer reproduce it.
