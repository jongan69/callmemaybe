Phone support, with consent and merchant control.

CallMeMaybe helps Shopify merchants handle customer-requested order-support calls and merchant-approved carrier traces. CALL-E handles the conversation; deterministic safeguards decide whether a call is allowed, and the merchant reviews every consequential next step.

Built with reviewable safety controls

Per-order consent, single-use identity verification, regional calling gates, suppression and quiet hours, completed-call billing, replay-resistant callbacks, PostgreSQL jobs, protected-data workflows, and fresh-order drift checks are part of the architecture.
